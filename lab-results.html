Product identity

The package and certificate should agree on the product name, tested form, cannabinoid strength, net contents, and lot code. Do not rely on a report for a different size, formula, or batch.

Report integrity

Look for the issuing laboratory, a unique sample or report identifier, received and report dates, and the test methods used. A direct laboratory verification link or QR code adds another useful check.

Testing scope

The relevant panels vary by product type and destination. A report may include cannabinoids, microbial contaminants, pesticides, heavy metals, residual solvents, mycotoxins, moisture, or other required analyses.

Labels and current requirements

Texas DSHS publishes current guidance for consumable hemp labeling and the consumable hemp program. Requirements can change and may differ by destination.
