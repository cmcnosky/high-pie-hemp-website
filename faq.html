Orders & shipping

Choose a product, select any available option, and add it to your cart. Checkout collects your contact and delivery information, then gives you an order number for follow-up.

Standard shipping is $7.95. Orders of $75 or more ship free. Final delivery timing and destination eligibility are confirmed with the order.

Contact customer care as soon as possible with your order number. Once an order has entered fulfillment, changes may no longer be possible.

Products & profiles

They are High Pie’s three signature profile families: Daybreak for sativa, Golden Hour for hybrid, and Nightfall for indica. The color system makes those families easy to spot across formats.
