Terms of use

High Pie products are intended for adults 21 and older. By using this site or placing an order, you confirm that you meet the age requirement and that your purchase and delivery are lawful where you live. Product eligibility can vary by destination.

Do not use intoxicating products before driving or operating machinery. Keep all products secured away from children and pets. Information on this site is not medical or legal advice.

Orders and payment

Prices are shown in US dollars. Checkout creates an order record using the products, quantities, contact details, and delivery information submitted by the customer. High Pie reviews the order and follows up by email with payment and delivery confirmation.

An order may be declined or adjusted when an item is unavailable, a destination is not eligible, required information is incomplete, or the order cannot be fulfilled as placed. Contact customer care promptly if an order needs to be changed or canceled.

Shipping and returns

Standard shipping is $7.95 and orders of $75 or more ship free. Available methods, estimated timing, and destination eligibility are confirmed with the order.

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete, or incorrect, contact hello@highpiehemp.com with the order number and clear photos of the package. Unopened return requests are reviewed individually; opened consumable products cannot be returned.

Product information

Always read and follow the package label. The package in hand controls for ingredients, warnings, net contents, cannabinoid strength, storage, and lot information. Product appearance can vary slightly between batches and screens.

To request a certificate of analysis, email the product name and lot code to hello@highpiehemp.com. Visit the lab-results guide for help matching a report.

Privacy

High Pie uses the contact and delivery information submitted at checkout to review, communicate about, and fulfill the order. This may include a name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order contents, and customer notes. Do not submit card numbers, passwords, government ID images, medical records, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Messages sent through the contact page are handled by the visitor's email provider and High Pie's email provider. Links to Instagram, government agencies, and other outside services are governed by those services' own terms and privacy practices.

Browser storage and external resources

The site stores the age confirmation and shopping cart in browser local storage so they remain available between pages and visits. Clearing site data removes those items. High Pie does not set advertising cookies on this site.

The pages request typefaces from Google Fonts, so the visitor's browser connects to Google to retrieve those resources. Standard connection information may be visible to that provider.

Contact, accessibility, and corrections

To request order help, report inaccurate information, ask a privacy question, or report an accessibility problem, visit the contact page or email hello@highpiehemp.com.
