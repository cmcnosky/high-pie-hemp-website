Our approach

High Pie is built around a simple idea: shopping for hemp should feel calm, coherent, and easy to understand. We organize the assortment by familiar formats and a profile system that stays consistent across gummies, flower, pre-rolls, and extracts.

Forest green and cream anchor the brand. Burnt tangerine, dusty rose, aubergine, and deep teal make each profile easy to spot. The result is one family of products that works together without every item looking the same.

Three signature profiles

Daybreak, Golden Hour, and Nightfall move through the rhythm of a day. They make it easier to browse while leaving room for personal preference, format, and flavor.

A complete shelf

Some days call for a ten-piece gummy box; others call for flower in clear glass, a seven-pack tin, a full-spectrum applicator, or a botanical salve. High Pie keeps those formats distinct while carrying the same visual language through the entire line.

Customer care

Product questions should have straightforward answers. Our safety guide, lab-results guide, shipping information, and customer-care channels are always available from the main navigation and footer.
